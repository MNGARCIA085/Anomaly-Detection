Optuna parallelism → parallel hyperparameter trials.
Hydra multirun → parallel independent experiments/configurations.

If your main goal is tuning AE hyperparameters, Optuna is enough.



--
Hydra multirun → parallelizes independent experiments/configurations.
Optuna n_jobs → parallelizes hyperparameter trials within a tuning experiment.

You can even combine them:

Hydra multirun
   │
   ├── AE tuning
   │      └── Optuna: 4 parallel trials
   │
   └── TransformerAE tuning
          └── Optuna: 4 parallel trials

But whether you should combine both levels of parallelism is another question, especially since you're CPU-only
